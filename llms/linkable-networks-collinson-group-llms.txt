# Linkable Networks (Collinson Group)

> Boston-based card-linked-offers (CLO) and SKU-linked-offers platform. Consumers attach digital offers to
> the credit and debit cards already in their wallet; when a linked card is used for a qualifying purchase
> on the Visa, MasterCard, American Express or PayPal rails, the Linkable platform receives the transaction,
> optionally confirms item-level detail via the on-premises Linkable SKU Matcher, and delivers the savings
> back as a statement credit, eGift, points or miles — with no POS integration, no extra hardware or
> software, and no punch cards.

## API

- [MyLinkables Consumer API](https://linkablenetworks.com/appweb-developers/): RESTful consumer API for
  offer display, offer linking, redemption tracking, consumer registration and consumer account
  management. Base URL `https://api.mylinkables.com`. Version 1.0. Responses in JSON or XML.

### Documented endpoints

- `POST consumerapi/consumer` — create a consumer
- `GET consumerapi/consumer` — view a consumer
- `DELETE consumerapi/consumer` — opt a consumer out
- `GET consumerapi/offers/available` — offers available to the consumer
- `GET consumerapi/offers/linked` — offers linked to a card
- `GET consumerapi/offers/redeemed` — redeemed offers
- `GET consumerapi/offers/reserved` — reserved offers
- `GET consumerapi/accounts` — list linked payment cards
- `DELETE consumerapi/accounts/${accountToken}` — remove a linked payment card
- Add and update card operations exist but their verbs and paths are not published. Card add/update is
  restricted to PCI-compliant partners.

### Authentication

OAuth 2.0 Resource Owner Password Credentials (RFC 6749 §4.3). Credentials are not self-service — partners
email Linkable Networks to receive documentation and credentials. No token endpoint, OAuth metadata
document or OpenID configuration is published.

## Products

- [Brand/SKU](https://linkablenetworks.com/brandsku/): product- and item-level promotions for brands and
  retailers, fundable with trade, co-op and MDF dollars.
- [SKU Matcher](https://linkablenetworks.com/sku-matcher/): application installed inside a merchant's
  firewall that locates the consumer's basket and reports back whether the offer's items are present.
- [Loyalty Programs](https://linkablenetworks.com/loyalty-programs/): single-purchase, multi-visit,
  SKU/item-level and always-on aggregate-spend reward mechanics layered on an existing loyalty program.
- [Developers/Publishers](https://linkablenetworks.com/appweb-developers/): the REST API for apps and
  websites distributing ecommerce and in-store redeemable offers.
- [MyLinkables](https://www.mylinkables.com/): the consumer-facing property.
